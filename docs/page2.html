§02 Mechanics

From deposit to redemption.

01

Wrap the asset

Deposit USDY into SYWrapper. You receive SY shares that track its value through the oracle.

02

Strip it

PrincipalManager takes custody of the SY and mints equal PT and YT for the chosen maturity.

03

Price the term

PT trades against SY in a pool whose curve follows time to maturity. YT routes through the same pool, so liquidity is never split.

04

Settle

At maturity PT redeems for its principal and YT pays what it accrued. Before then, PT and YT recombine into SY.

§05 Compliance

Compliance travels with the asset.

Every SY, PT, YT and LP position inherits the controls the issuer already runs on its Stellar Asset Contract. No second allow-list to drift out of sync.

Market creation

Only the issuer can open a market.

Initialisation requires admin == SAC.admin(), read live. Maturity and fees are set by the asset's own administrator.

Authorization

Ineligible wallets cannot hold PT or YT.

Deposits, mints, transfers and trades check the underlying SAC's authorized() for sender and recipient.

Recovery

Clawback without contagion.

A deauthorized account's positions move to RecoveryEscrow, stay fully backed, settle at maturity and return to the issuer's native clawback. Other holders are untouched.

Deauthorize→RecoveryEscrow→Held to maturity→Settle in USDY→Clawback
