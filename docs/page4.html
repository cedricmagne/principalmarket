How it works

From deposit to redemption in four steps.

01 · Wrap

Deposit the RWA

Deposit USDY into SYWrapper and receive SY shares that track the asset's value through the oracle.

02 · Split

Mint PT and YT

PrincipalManager takes custody of SY and mints equal PT and YT for the chosen maturity.

03 · Trade

Price the term

Trade PT against SY in the time-aware pool. YT routes through the same pool with a flash mint, so liquidity is never split.

04 · Settle

Redeem or claim

At maturity PT redeems for its principal in USDY and YT pays the yield it accrued. Before then, PT + YT recombine into SY.

Compliance architecture

Compliance travels with the asset.

Every SY, PT, YT and LP position inherits the controls the issuer already runs on the underlying Stellar Asset Contract. There is no separate allow-list to drift out of sync.

The issuer creates the market

Initialisation requires admin == SAC.admin(), read live. Maturity and fees are set by the asset's own administrator.

Authorization is inherited

Deposits, mints, transfers and trades check the underlying SAC's authorized() for sender and recipient. An ineligible wallet cannot hold PT or YT.

Recovery without contagion

A deauthorized account's positions move to RecoveryEscrow, stay fully backed, settle at maturity and return to the issuer's native clawback. Other holders are untouched.

Protocol

Ten Soroban contracts. Open source.

SEP-41 tokens, typed errors and storage tiers throughout. Oracle reads carry freshness checks, a fallback source and a ±10% deviation guard; a rolling 24-hour circuit breaker can pause any market.
